# Briefing

Start from `slides.md` in this folder. People expect a cover, the point of the meeting, one idea per slide, and a close.

## How to fill it

- **Title** names the subject and the audience or date. The subheading says why this meeting exists.
- **Purpose** is one content slide: the decision, update, or question. Not an agenda of ten items.
- **Body slides** each carry one idea. A heading plus at most five bullets, or two short sentences.
- **Quote**, when the source has a line worth hearing in the person's words. Skip it when you would have to invent one.
- **Closing** states the ask or the next step. Do not introduce a new idea there.
- Label assumptions. Replace every "Replace with" placeholder.
